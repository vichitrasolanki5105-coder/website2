Welcome to Animal World

Animals are an important part of our environment. They live in forests, oceans, deserts and many other places.

Animal Information

Animals are multicellular, eukaryotic organisms in the kingdom Animalia. With few exceptions, animals consume organic material, breathe oxygen, are able to move, reproduce sexually, and grow from a hollow sphere of cells (the blastula) during embryonic development.
